Zenyomi keeps your manga and your anime in one app, instead of two.

It is built on Mihon, so everything the manga side does is what Mihon does, and anime support is added alongside it rather than in place of it.

Features:
* Read manga, webtoons and comics with customizable viewing modes: right-to-left, left-to-right, and long strip for webtoons.
* Watch anime from the same app, with its own library and its own sources.
* Organize both collections into custom categories, with unread badges and automatic updates when something new drops.
* Sync your progress with services like MyAnimeList, AniList, Kitsu, MangaBaka and more.
* Download for offline use and back up your whole library and settings for safe keeping or device migration.
* A Material You interface with full theme customization, including a strict True Dark mode.

Zenyomi hosts no content of its own. Sources come from third-party extensions that are not affiliated with the project.
