Fixed: the skip now lands five seconds before the opening ends, and no longer past it. The end of an opening is a handover, not a frame, so the button leaves that margin and the episode picks up there instead of on a scene already under way. Those five seconds are counted from where the opening really ends: AniSkip's times were measured on another copy of the episode — a few seconds of logo more, a cut made differently — so they came out shifted and the jump landed past the end, taking episode with it. That difference between copies is now subtracted too, capped, and chapters from the file itself do not need it because they describe the copy being played.

Fixed: skipping the opening no longer costs you episode for taking a moment to press. When nothing says where the opening ends, the button used to jump the configured length from wherever it was pressed — press it ten seconds into the opening and you landed ten seconds past its end, and those ten seconds of episode were gone.

An opening is 85 seconds long counting from where it starts, so that is where it is now measured from: with no data for the episode, the assumption is the one the button already makes — that the opening starts with the episode — so pressing at second five and at second fifty land in the same place. It can leave a few seconds of opening playing on an episode that opens on a scene, which is the right way round to be wrong. And it never lands past the last second of the episode, which could previously run straight into the next one.
