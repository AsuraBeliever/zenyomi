Changed: the skip opening button only shows up when something actually knows where the opening is. It used to be there always — no chapters in the file and no answer from AniSkip, and it still appeared on the first frame offering a fixed jump. On an anime that opens on a three minute scene that was a button lying about what it does, and pressing it landed in the middle of the episode. Now the opening is skipped with the seek bar in those episodes, and the button that does appear always means what it says.

Removed: the "Opening length" setting, which only sized that blind jump.

Improved: more anime get exact opening times. Identifying the anime by its title failed whenever a source and AniList wrote it differently without meaning anything different — "4th Season" against "Season 4" against a plain "4", "Bouken-roku" against "Boukenroku", "Gin Tama" against "Gintama" — and whenever AniList's search gave up on a forty character light novel title. None of that loosens the rule: the words still have to be the same words in the same order. Measured over 105 real library titles, 73% to 85%; over 485 anime with their id known in advance, 459 correct to 469, and not one resolved to the wrong anime.
