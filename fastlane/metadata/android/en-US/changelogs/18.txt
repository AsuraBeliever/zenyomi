Fixed: a series with a thousand episodes no longer bogs the app down. Opening One Piece was doing about three thousand storage lookups on the drawing thread and rebuilding the whole list three times over.

Fixed: episodes have sound. Android 16 was muting the app because the player never claimed the audio output. Also holds a real buffer for sources that send the audio as a separate stream, so it no longer runs dry and stutters.

Fixed: the player shows that it is loading instead of a black screen.

Changed: an anime entry now looks like a manga entry — backdrop, author and status, action row, description with genre chips, filter and sort, batch download, and a play button that continues where you left off. It also fetches its own description and genres on first open.
