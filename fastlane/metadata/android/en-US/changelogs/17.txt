Zenyomi 0.8.2

Video from an online source plays. Until now only a local file did: the headers a
source resolves a video with were dropped on the way to the player, so hosts that
check where a request came from refused it and the screen stayed black.

Subtitles appear, including the ones a source ships as a separate file, and the
preferred audio and subtitle language setting now picks the track it promised.

Dragging the seek bar no longer freezes the app.

Searching works on sources that were failing before a single request left the
device, KickAssAnime among them. A search that finds nothing says so instead of
claiming the source failed, and an episode from a source that has stopped working
says which.
